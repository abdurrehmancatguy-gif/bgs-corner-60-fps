01The House

Oud is not a scent. It is patience, distilled.

Born on a quiet corner of Dubai, BGS CORNER is devoted to a single material: oud — the dark, resinous heart of the agar tree, rarer by weight than gold.

We buy standing trees, not bottled promises. Our ouds are distilled the old way and left to sleep in the dark until they turn from smoke to silk. Then, and only then, they are poured into full-lead crystal cut like a jewel — one flacon at a time, each sealed by hand in 24-karat gold leaf.

“A great oud is not blended. It is waited for.”
The House — Dubaï, 2026

02The Oud

From forest to flacon.

I

The Wood

Wild agarwood from old-growth forests of Assam and Cambodia, graded by hand, by nose, by fire.

II

The Fire

Slow hydro-distillation in copper stills — days of low flame for a few precious tolas.

III

The Sleep

The oil rests in darkness for up to twelve years, shedding smoke, gaining silk and honey.

IV

The Pour

Into crystal cut like a diamond, sealed in gold leaf, signed by the maître who poured it.

03The Collection

Three ouds. One signature.

Oud Impérial

Twelve-year Assam oud, dense and regal, lifted by a thread of saffron light.

Oud Ambré

The desert at dusk — warm resin, sweet wind, a rose that refuses night.

Oud Cristal

Light through dark glass — a veil of iris over the palest oud we own.
